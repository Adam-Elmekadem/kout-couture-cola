# Couture-Cola / Brand system

## Position

**Idea:** A familiar pop feeling, recut as contemporary streetwear. The brand mixes the confidence of a mass-culture icon with tactile fashion detail. The clothes lead; the reference is a wink.

**Audience:** Style-conscious shoppers and creative portfolio viewers who like oversized silhouettes, collectible drops, and concise visual stories.

**Line:** “The familiar, recut.”

**Voice:** Short, physical, confident. Talk about weight, cut, texture, and movement. Avoid beverage puns in purchase language and avoid claiming a real collaboration.

## Identity

- **Primary name:** Couture-Cola (capitalized in prose; `COUTURE-COLA` in navigational lockups).
- **Display wordmark:** Oversized stacked block capitals. Use it as typography, not as a script imitation.
- **Monogram:** Original interlocking geometric CC in `public/cc-mark.svg`. Its heavy square cuts echo varsity patches.
- **Signature gesture:** One broad lacquer-red paint stroke, used only at campaign scale or as an apparel appliqué. This is the single expressive layer against a restrained page.
- **Avoid:** The original beverage script, bottle silhouette, exact can layout, slogan structure, white ribbon, or any implication of an official partnership.

| Warm line | `#D8CDC2` | Quiet separators |

Keep most of the shopping area neutral. Red is a garment color and a deliberate accent, never the color of every control.

## Type

- **Display:** Impact / condensed heavy sans fallback, tightly set, monumental scale. The large wordmark intentionally clashes with the loose painted background.
- **Interface and body:** Arial/Helvetica sans, 11–17 px for navigation, product labels, and body text.
- **Rules:** No cursive brand lettering. No unnecessary all-caps micro-labels. Keep paragraphs brief and product names easy to scan.

## Layout and motion

- The opening is full bleed, image led, and typographic. Product browsing shifts into a spacious cream field.
- A single editorial portrait interrupts the grid; modular fabric panels close the story.
- Hover feedback should show an action or bring a garment closer. No continuous parallax or auto-playing motion.
- Maintain visible focus, 44 px targets, native dialogs, image alt text, and reduced-motion support.
